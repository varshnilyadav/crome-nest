Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 20, 2026

Welcome to Crome Nest ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our website or book our car detailing services in Hyderabad.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when scheduling an appointment or contacting us. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone number, and email address.
  • Vehicle Information: Car make, model, year, and service preferences.
  • Appointment Preferences: Desired booking date and time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To schedule, manage, and execute your detailing service appointments.
  • To communicate with you regarding your bookings, reminders, and customer service requests.
  • To redirect and pre-fill booking details via WhatsApp for convenient client communication.
  • To send occasional promotional updates or seasonal offers (with your explicit consent).

3. WhatsApp Integration

Our website utilizes a WhatsApp redirection feature to simplify booking schedules. When you submit our scheduling form, key appointment details are encoded into a WhatsApp message link. Clicking this button opens WhatsApp to transmit the message to our official studio support number (+91 95156 66832). Your conversation details on WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, lease, or rent your personal information to third parties. We take appropriate measures to secure your data from unauthorized access or modification. We only share details with trusted team members who require access to complete your vehicle's detailing services.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use basic technical cookies to optimize loading times, keep navigation settings, and track basic anonymous user interactions via website statistics tools to improve user experience.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to incorrect data, or request the complete deletion of your booking history from our records. Please contact us via phone or WhatsApp at +91 95156 66832 to make a request.

7. Updates to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will take effect immediately upon posting to this page. We encourage you to check this page periodically for updates.
