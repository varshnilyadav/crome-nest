Who We Are

Redefining Automotive Shine

Established in 2022 in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, Crome Nest was founded with a singular purpose: to bring world-class surface restoration and automotive protection to discerning car owners. We don't believe in standard commercial washing. For us, every vehicle represents a canvas that deserves precision correction, flawless finishes, and the absolute highest level of protection.

Whether you drive a daily luxury sedan, a high-torque performance sports car, or a classic utility truck, our certified detailers employ specialized tools, temperature-controlled clean rooms, and imported nano-compounds to elevate its aesthetics.

Our Mission

To preserve the look and value of our clients' vehicles through meticulous workmanship, top-tier products, and cutting-edge detailing techniques. We aim to establish Crome Nest as the premier authority in auto styling and paint protection, offering unmatched durability and breathtaking mirror finishes.

Our Vision

To lead the Indian automotive detailing industry by setting new benchmarks for quality, customer care, and technological adaptation. We envision a space where car detailing becomes an art form, accessible to enthusiasts who demand perfection without compromise.

Our Beliefs

Core Company Values

Absolute Precision

We work down to the micrometer, inspecting paint corrections under specialized multi-spectrum lights to eliminate 100% of swirls.

Uncompromised Quality

We source only industry-leading protective TPU films and nano-coatings directly from elite manufacturers in the USA and Japan.

Honesty & trust

No hidden charges, no unnecessary upsells. We explain every step of the correction process and provide written warranty certification.

Automotive Passion

We treat every supercar, SUV, and daily driver with the exact same respect, care, and enthusiasm as if it were our own.
