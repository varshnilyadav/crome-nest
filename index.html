What We Do

Our Detailing Services

01. Surface Restoration

Exterior Detailing & paint Correction

Swirl marks, scratch webs, and oxidation dull your car's natural color. Our multi-stage paint correction process uses professional-grade compounding and polishers to shave off microscopic imperfections, bringing out a stunning, deep, reflection-rich mirror finish.

Multi-stage orbital buffing
Safely removes 85%+ swirls
Iron and fallout decontamination
Clay bar paint cleansing
Book Detailing
02. Cabin Rejuvenation

Interior Deep Cleaning

Step into a showroom-fresh cabin. We go beyond vacuuming. Our detailing specialists utilize high-temperature steam sanitizers, pH-balanced leather conditioners, and fine detail brushes to clean every vent, crevice, dashboard section, and fabric fiber.

140°C deep steam extraction
Leather cleaning & conditioning
Odor elimination & sanitization
Meticulous dashboard & vents
Book Cleaning
03. Shield & Shimmer

Nano-Ceramic Coatings

Protect your paint with a glass-like shell. Our premium nano-ceramic coatings bond molecularly with your car's clear coat. This creates a hard hydrophobic layer that repels dirt, acid rain, bird droppings, and UV damage, reducing maintenance wash frequency by 50%.

9H/10H extreme hardness rating
Intense hydrophobic water beading
Permanent glass gloss look
3+ years written warranty
Book Ceramic Coating
04. Self-Healing Shield

Paint Protection Film (PPF)

The ultimate physical defense against road hazards. Our premium clear TPU Paint Protection Film absorbs stone chips, road debris impacts, key marks, and minor parking scrapes. Under light heat (like sunlight or hot water), minor swirls on the film heal themselves automatically.

Premium TPU high-elastic film
Self-healing heat technology
Complete UV & scratch barrier
Up to 10 years warranty
Book PPF Protection
05. Metal Polish

Chrome & Rim Restoration

Brake dust, iron filings, and road salts quickly oxidize chrome exhaust tips and alloy wheels. Our wheel restoration process clears caked-on contaminants, cleans deep inside the barrels, and machine polishes metal elements back to a dazzling, reflective sheen.

Full brake dust removal
Chrome metal oxidation correction
Rim sealing protection coating
Exhaust tips restoration
Book Wheel Polish
06. Premium Maintenance

Full Car Spa & Snow Foam Wash

The ultimate clean-up ritual. Far superior to basic car washes, our full spa sequence uses pH-neutral snow foams that encapsulate dirt without scratching, combined with multi-bucket wash methods, deep engine bay dusting, window claying, and paint sealant protection.

Meticulous multi-bucket foam wash
Deep clean engine bay dressing
Glass clay bar prep & wax layer
Tyre conditioning & dressing
Book Car Spa
